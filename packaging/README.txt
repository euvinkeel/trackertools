trackertools
============

To start it: double-click trackertools.exe.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info",
then "Run anyway". (Windows says that about programs that don't come from a big
company.)

Keep all the files in this folder together: ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe are what
read your videos.


Updates
-------
When a new version is out, a button appears at the top of the window:
"Update to ...". Click it: trackertools downloads the new version, saves your
work, and restarts by itself. You can also look in the Settings tab, under
Updates, and click "Check for updates".


Your work
---------
Projects save by themselves, one per video, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\trackertools
(Settings tab, Files, "Open the folder").


ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe are FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), under the GPL. Its
license is in FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt; its source code is at
https://ffmpeg.org/download.html.
